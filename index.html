Private by design

🔐

Strong, modern encryption

Every file is encrypted individually before it is ever written to storage.

🫆

Biometric unlock

The key lives in your device's secure hardware and is released only after a successful biometric check. A PIN is offered on devices without biometrics.

🗂️

Sorted automatically

Photos, Documents, Financial, Medical, Videos and Other — each file is categorized on import.

📡

Device-to-device transfer

Send or stream a file to another of your devices over local Wi-Fi. Never through the internet. Free.

📱

Unlock your computer with your phone

For desktops without a fingerprint reader. Pairing is verified with a code on both devices.

🚫

No account, no cloud, no tracking

No sign-in, ads, analytics or servers. Nothing about you or your files is ever uploaded.

Free, with one optional upgrade

The free plan holds up to 10 files. BioVault Premium is a one-time purchase — no subscription — that removes the cap. Because there is no cloud backup, a lost or reset device means the vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
